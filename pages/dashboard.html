feature#191@grok2026-11-01

Support optional early Bluetooth in initramfs for LUKS passphrase entry

Known Omarchy limitation: Bluetooth stack (modules, firmware, BlueZ) loads after LUKS unlock, so BT keyboards/mice can't enter the passphrase (same as BIOS). DHH documented it; use wired or Logitech Bolt/Unifying dongle (USB HID, works early). Community workaround exists: AUR mkinitcpio-bluetooth + add bluetooth hook before encrypt, uhid module, needed firmware, then limine-mkinitcpio -P. Some users successfully unlock with MX Keys this way. Not shipped (PR closed); hardware-specific and bloats initramfs.
